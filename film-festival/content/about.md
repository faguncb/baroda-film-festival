---

Cinema moves through every other art. It reflects the human mind, one person at a time and as a public.

To cultivate a habit of looking at cinema with care, and to give young, independent filmmakers a place to be seen, [The Film Society Of Vadodara](https://www.instagram.com/thefilmsocietyvadodara) and [Film Club of Baroda](https://www.instagram.com/filmclubofbaroda) joined in a common cause. From that collaboration, the Baroda Film Festival was born.

This is our second edition. It widens in the number of films, in the conversations around them, and in who takes part.

Come and sit with the work. Here, cinema is something to be experienced.
