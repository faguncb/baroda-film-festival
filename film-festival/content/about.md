Cinema moves through every other art. It reflects the human mind, one person at a time and as a public.

To cultivate a habit of looking at cinema with care, and to give young, independent filmmakers a place to be seen, [The Film Society Of Vadodara](https://www.instagram.com/thefilmsocietyvadodara) and [Film Club of Baroda](https://www.instagram.com/filmclubofbaroda) joined in a common cause. From that collaboration, the Baroda Film Festival was born.

This is our second edition. It widens in the number of films, in the conversations around them, and in who takes part.

## Film Club of Baroda

[Film Club of Baroda](https://www.instagram.com/filmclubofbaroda) is a community of cinema lovers who believe that films are more than just entertainment — they are stories, conversations, and experiences that bring people together.

From film screenings and interactive quizzes to workshops and engaging discussions, the club celebrates the art and craft of cinema.

Our aim is to build a vibrant community of cinephiles, encourage meaningful conversations around films, and create a space where cinema is experienced beyond the screen. The Film Club of Baroda independently did a Children's Film Festival in 2025 and, along with [The Film Society of Vadodara](https://www.instagram.com/thefilmsocietyvadodara), co-hosted the first edition of the Baroda Film Festival 2025.

Because great films don't just deserve to be watched. They deserve to be discussed, debated, and celebrated.

## The Film Society of Vadodara

[The Film Society of Vadodara](https://www.instagram.com/thefilmsocietyvadodara) (TFSV) is a community where people from all walks of life gather to savour the taste of world cinema.

In order to revive the cultural spirit of the city, a bunch of world cinema lovers started movie screenings — genre and language, absolutely no bar — followed by discussions that give everyone a timeout from their mobile screen time and the daily routine. For three years we have been running the show on every alternate weekend, bringing both classics and independent cinema to our audience. Indie filmmakers from the city, the state, and the country have been encouraged to showcase their films.

Apart from co-organising the Baroda Film Festival with [Film Club of Baroda](https://www.instagram.com/filmclubofbaroda), we were quite instrumental in bringing independent documentaries on water conservation to the Dastak Paani Festival.
